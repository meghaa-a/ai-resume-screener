About Our System

The AI Resume Screening System simplifies and automates the hiring process using advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning techniques.

It intelligently analyzes resumes, extracts key skills, calculates similarity scores, ranks candidates, and highlights missing skills — helping recruiters make faster and smarter decisions.

Whether you're a recruiter or a job seeker, this system helps you understand candidate potential beyond just resumes.
